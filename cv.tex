    \item[--] \lead{Benchmark audit} showed that leading public prompt-injection benchmarks (LLMail, AgentHarm, TensorTrust, SafeGuard) can be separated by surface features alone: a bag-of-words baseline scores near-perfect AUC (up to 1.00), and three are separable by message length.
